## Book Structure Overview

The outline follows a progression from core theory to advanced application, mirroring how established trading curricula (from beginner S&R modules to full SMC/ICT integration) are typically structured. It also incorporates specific SNR sub-methodologies like Malaysian SNR and MSNR (Market Structure + SNR) that are widely taught in forex communities, since these give your book technical depth beyond generic "support and resistance" content. ## Part 1: Foundations

- **Chapter 1 — Introduction to Markets and Price Action.** What moves price, market participants, why S&R exists (buyer/seller pressure zones, not just lines). [ultimamarkets](https://www.ultimamarkets.com/ms/academy/snr-forex/)
- **Chapter 2 — Chart Reading Basics.** Candlestick anatomy, timeframes, bar vs. line vs. candle charts. [storage.googleapis](https://storage.googleapis.com/teachmint/public/48150e0a-efff-4019-be11-43f5ee019011.pdf)
- **Chapter 3 — What Is SNR (Support & Resistance)?** Definition, psychology behind price reversals, zones vs. lines concept. [ultimamarkets](https://www.ultimamarkets.com/ms/academy/snr-forex/)
- **Chapter 4 — Market Structure Fundamentals.** Swing highs/lows, HH/HL/LH/LL, trend vs. range vs. reversal. [scribd](https://www.scribd.com/document/904602258/Complete-Trading-Syllabus)

## Part 2: Core SNR Theory

- **Chapter 5 — Identifying SNR Levels.** Two-candle rule (sell+buy = support, buy+sell = resistance), marking by close/open, ignoring wicks. [scribd](https://www.scribd.com/document/952759594/Msnr-Free-Guide)
- **Chapter 6 — Malaysian SNR Method.** Drawing "V" and "A" shapes on line charts, open/close-based zone construction. [scribd](https://www.scribd.com/document/846474911/Malaysian-Snr-Theory)
- **Chapter 7 — Fresh vs. Unfresh SNR.** Untapped liquidity zones vs. tested levels, why freshness matters for probability. [studocu](https://www.studocu.vn/vn/document/dai-hoc-kinh-te-quoc-dan/acca-sbr-book-2019/msnr-technical-analysis-guide-candlestick-snr-strategies/149650734)
- **Chapter 8 — Zones, Not Lines.** Why S&R should be drawn as areas, choosing the right timeframe (H4/Daily for zones). [scribd](https://www.scribd.com/document/1003958147/Complete-SNR-and-MSNR-Strategy-Guide)
- **Chapter 9 — Role Reversal Levels.** SBR (support-become-resistance) and RBS (resistance-become-support). [studocu](https://www.studocu.com/row/document/kisii-university/special-education/akfx-malaysian-snr-understanding-support-and-resistance-dynamics/157799406)

## Part 3: MSNR and Confluence Trading

- **Chapter 10 — Market Structure + SNR (MSNR).** Combining trend structure with key zones for higher-probability setups. [scribd](https://www.scribd.com/document/1003958147/Complete-SNR-and-MSNR-Strategy-Guide)
- **Chapter 11 — Multi-Timeframe (MTF) Analysis.** Aligning HTF bias with LTF entries, storyline concept. [studocu](https://www.studocu.vn/vn/document/dai-hoc-kinh-te-quoc-dan/acca-sbr-book-2019/msnr-technical-analysis-guide-candlestick-snr-strategies/149650734)
- **Chapter 12 — Confluence Factors.** Trendlines, candlestick confirmation, momentum indicators, liquidity sweeps at SNR zones. [scribd](https://www.scribd.com/document/846474911/Malaysian-Snr-Theory)
- **Chapter 13 — SNR + Smart Money Concepts (SMC/ICT).** Order blocks, liquidity, and how SNR zones relate to institutional footprints (ties directly to your ICT/SMC background).
