# Chapter 14: Buy Setups at Support

## 14.1 Opening Part 4

Parts 1–3 built the complete analytical toolkit: price action, SNR mechanics, MSNR structure filtering, multi-timeframe analysis, confluence, and SMC/ICT integration. Part 4 now converts that toolkit into precise, repeatable execution rules. This chapter covers the first and most fundamental setup type: **buying at a qualified support zone**.

## 14.2 Pre-Entry Checklist

Before considering any buy setup, confirm every item below — this is the complete distillation of Chapters 1–13 into a single actionable checklist:

1. **HTF structure** (Chapter 4) shows an uptrend (HH/HL) or the setup is at the bottom of a confirmed range (Chapter 10.6).
2. **Zone is properly constructed** (Chapters 5, 8) using the two-candle handoff and body-based boundaries.
3. **Zone passes the MSNR filter** (Chapter 10) — aligned with structure, not counter-trend.
4. **Zone is fresh or, at most, once-tested** (Chapter 7), unless it's a qualifying role-reversal zone (Chapter 9).
5. **MTF confirms a corrective approach**, not an MSS invalidating the uptrend (Chapter 11).
6. **At least one confluence factor** is present per the Chapter 12/13 checklists (trendline, candlestick confirmation, liquidity sweep, FVG, or discount-zone location).

## 14.3 Entry Trigger

Once price enters the qualified zone on your LTF chart (M15/M5, per Chapter 11), wait for one of these specific triggers before entering:

- **Two-candle handoff** (Chapter 5) — bearish candle followed by a decisive bullish candle inside the zone.
- **Rejection candle** — a pin bar or hammer with a long lower wick and small body, closing back above the zone's lower boundary.
- **Liquidity sweep reversal** (Chapter 13) — a brief spike below the zone followed by a sharp close back inside it.

Never enter simply because price has *reached* the zone — always wait for one of these explicit triggers to confirm buyers have actually taken control at that specific moment.

## 14.4 Stop-Loss Placement

Place your stop-loss just beyond the zone's outer boundary (Chapter 8), not at an arbitrary fixed distance:

- If using a standard zone, place the stop a small buffer below the zone's lowest boundary to account for minor noise, sized relative to current ATR (Chapter 8.5).
- If entering off a liquidity sweep, place the stop below the sweep's low, since that low has already demonstrated where the market pushed to grab liquidity — a reasonable worst-case invalidation point.

## 14.5 Take-Profit Targets

Take-profit levels should reference the *next* significant structural point, not an arbitrary risk-reward multiple alone:

- **First target** — the most recent swing high (Chapter 4), a natural point where opposing resistance may appear.
- **Second target** — the next significant HTF resistance zone identified using Chapter 5's marking method.
- Consider partial profit-taking at the first target and trailing the remainder toward the second, a practical technique expanded in Chapter 19 (Risk Management).

## 14.6 Position Sizing Based on Confluence Score

Referencing the confluence checklist from Chapter 12.7, scale your position size to setup quality:

- **Mandatory MSNR pass only, 0–1 confluence points** — minimum size or skip.
- **2 confluence points** — standard planned risk size.
- **3+ confluence points** (as in the Chapter 13 worked example) — maximum planned risk size within your predefined risk limits (Chapter 19).

- HTF: Daily uptrend confirmed (HH/HL sequence).
- Zone: Fresh support at $2,362–$2,368, formed via a clean two-candle handoff, aligned with the latest Higher Low.
- MSNR: Passes — support in the direction structure favors.
- MTF: H1 shows a corrective pullback into the zone, no MSS present.

## 14.8 Chapter Summary

- A valid buy setup requires passing a full pre-entry checklist covering structure, zone quality, freshness, MTF confirmation, and confluence.
- Entries are triggered only by specific candlestick or liquidity-sweep confirmation inside the zone — never on approach alone.
- Stops belong just beyond the zone's outer boundary; targets reference the next structural swing point or HTF zone.
- Position size should scale directly with your confluence score, not remain fixed across every trade.
